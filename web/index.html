Why use a blockchain for workshop seats?

A single organizer can do this more simply in a database. This experiment is for independent organizers who agree on a shared pool and rules, but do not want either party to control the entire process. Both approve the fixed pool. Every participant signs the same plan. The contract checks all conditions and updates all seats in one transaction.

Matching and database transactions already exist. Our focus is safe coordination across organizers, with an outcome anyone can inspect.

What is real, and what still needs a pilot?

The home demo is a simulation. The wallet page connects to Monad Testnet and requires actual wallet approvals. Published testnet proofs use fictional contract-wallet actors controlled by one test fixture; they are not independent human signatures.

  • Seats are non-tradable records: no token, payment, resale, or NFT.
  • Withdrawal takes effect if its transaction is included before the relay executes.
  • A relay can add someone when it ends at a vacancy. A simple cycle does not increase attendance.
  • Real organizers, identity checks, and offline fulfillment need a future pilot. No real-user study has been run.
